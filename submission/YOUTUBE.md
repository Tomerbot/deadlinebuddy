Title: DeadlineBuddy: a simulated Alexa+ that plans your deadlines (Amazon Developer Hackathon)
Visibility: Public (the rules require a public video)
Made for kids: No

Description:
DeadlineBuddy is a simulated Alexa+ experience for students. Say what's due, and it plans every day before the deadline, remembers your week, and re-plans when you fall behind.
Live demo: https://tomerbot.github.io/deadlinebuddy/
Code: https://github.com/Tomerbot/deadlinebuddy
Built for the Build, Ship, Shape: Amazon Developer Hackathon (Alexa+ track). Built with Claude.
